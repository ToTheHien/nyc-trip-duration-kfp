<objective>
Build out the ingest boundary into its full two-tier form: chunked reads that never materialise a whole month at once (REQ-C4), a counted-and-logged row-level quality pre-filter, and a pandera schema with real Checks that hard-fails a structurally drifted month (REQ-C1, D-09, D-09a).

Purpose: D-09 as literally written would reject every one of the twelve real months, because RESEARCH.md measured a ~1% baseline rate of non-positive `trip_distance` and a steady trickle of placeholder zone IDs in every sampled month. D-09a's amendment splits enforcement so that routine per-row noise is filtered and counted while genuine schema drift still fails loudly. Getting that boundary right is what makes REQ-C1's "fails loudly" mean something rather than firing on every normal month.

Output: expanded `lib/ingest.py` and `lib/schemas.py`, their two dedicated test modules, and a verification that all 12 downloaded months pass.
</objective>
