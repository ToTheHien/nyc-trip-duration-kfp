Harden `lib/features.py`'s distance path.

Keep `haversine_km(lat1, lon1, lat2, lon2) -> pd.Series` entirely on numpy ufuncs over whole Series — `np.radians`, `np.sin`, `np.cos`, `np.arcsin`, `np.sqrt`. Clamp the arcsin argument into the closed interval 0.0 to 1.0 with `np.clip` before the square root: at zero separation, floating-point cancellation can leave the radicand marginally negative, which would surface as a NaN distance for the perfectly ordinary case of a trip that starts and ends in the same zone.
